# Design System Inspired by Montamont

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Montamont embodies refined Alpine hospitality through a design system rooted in timeless elegance and understated sophistication. The visual language draws from mountain landscapes—earthy, natural, and serene—with a deliberate restraint that prioritizes content and imagery over decorative flourish. The system favors expansive whitespace, dramatic photography, and a monochromatic foundation punctuated by deep charcoal and black accents. Typography is deliberately varied between two families: the geometric precision of ATC Diatype conveys contemporary luxury, while the lighter, classical GT Alpina adds human warmth to secondary elements. Every interaction is intentional; the design avoids visual noise in favor of clarity and contemplation, inviting users to explore curated Alpine destinations with the confidence of an insider's guide.

**Key Characteristics**

- Monochromatic palette anchored in near-white canvas (`#F9F7F5`) and deep charcoal (`#1D1D1C`) with strategic black (`#000000`) for contrast
- Large, dynamic typography with generous line spacing and fine letter-spacing control
- Sharp edges (border-radius `{rounded.none}` on most components) with occasional subtle rounding on interactive elements
- Minimalist component styling—no gradients, no heavy shadows, color blocking via surface changes
- Photography as hero narrative; layouts prioritize image prominence over UI chrome
- Dual typeface system: geometric ATC Diatype for headings and body emphasis; classical GT Alpina for navigation and secondary text
- Generous section spacing (`{spacing.band}` = 68px) creates breathing room and content hierarchy
- High-contrast interactive elements (white buttons on dark backgrounds, dark text on light)
- Responsive typography that scales proportionally across viewport sizes

### Principles

- **Contrast through size, not color.** The system relies on generous scaling and weight to create visual hierarchy rather than color shifts. Body copy remains dark on light backgrounds throughout.
- **Geometric precision with classical warmth.** ATC Diatype (geometric sans) is paired with GT Alpina (classical serif) to balance contemporary sophistication with editorial tradition.
- **Tracked headings and buttons.** Many display and button sizes employ explicit letter-spacing to reinforce geometric character and prevent visual density.
- **Spacious line-height.** Even small text maintains line-heights ≥ 1.0, ensuring legibility and a sense of openness. Display sizes compress to 0.95 for dramatic impact.
- **Monospace for metadata.** All code, labels, and uppercase technical text use mono families with increased tracking to signal precision and separation from prose.
- **Font weight as emphasis.** Rather than introducing new colors, the system uses 300 (light) for secondary navigation, 400 (regular) for body, and 700 (bold) for CTA and headings.

### Spacing System

The system uses a base unit of 4px, scaled across 10 named intervals:
- `{spacing.xxs}` = 4px — Micro-spacing between adjacent elements
- `{spacing.xs}` = 8px — Tight component padding
- `{spacing.sm}` = 12px — Small margin or padding
- `{spacing.md}` = 16px — Standard margin; mid-level spacing
- `{spacing.lg}` = 20px — Generous component padding
- `{spacing.xl}` = 28px — Large section margin
- `{spacing.xxl}` = 36px — Extra-large separation
- `{spacing.xxxl}` = 48px — Maximum component padding
- `{spacing.section}` = 56px — Between-section vertical spacing
- `{spacing.band}` = 68px — Full-width band separation; defines rhythm of major content blocks

**Usage Context:**
- **Component Interior:** `{spacing.xs}` to `{spacing.lg}` for button padding, input fields, badge spacing.
- **Section Margins:** `{spacing.section}` (56px) between content sections; `{spacing.band}` (68px) for full-width block transitions.
- **Typography Spacing:** Heading-to-body spacing typically uses `{spacing.md}` to `{spacing.lg}`; body-to-body uses `{spacing.sm}`.

### Grid & Container

- **Max Width:** 1371px (measured at 1440px viewport)
- **Content Column:** Scales from 340px (375px viewport) to 1371px (1440px viewport)
- **Grid Columns:** 3 columns at 1024px and above; 4 columns at 768px and below (observed at 375px and 768px)
- **Section Padding Horizontal:** 0px measured at 375px and 768px; 0px at 1024px+ (container-based layout, not edge-padded)
- **Layout Pattern:** Full-width hero images with centered text overlay; multi-column content grid below

### Whitespace Philosophy

Montamont uses whitespace as a primary design tool. Large gaps between sections (`{spacing.band}` = 68px) create visual breathing room and invite contemplation. Heading-to-body spacing is generous (`{spacing.lg}` = 20px), avoiding visual cramping. The canvas background (`#F9F7F5`) is warm and soft, allowing generous whitespace to feel intimate rather than empty. Horizontal padding is minimal at small viewports (0px), allowing typography and imagery to dominate without frame decoration. This philosophy reinforces the brand's position as a curated guide: every element earns its space.

### Border Radius Scale

- `{rounded.none}` = 0px — Cards, images, modals, overlays, badges (on dark), inputs, links. Sharp edges reinforce geometric precision and architectural linearity.
- `{rounded.xs}` = 5.14286px — Buttons, badges (light variant). Subtle roundness signals interactivity without softening the overall aesthetic.
- `{rounded.sm}` = 6.85714px — Form elements (measured at 6px). Minimal softening to distinguish inputs from static content.

**Component Assignment:**
- Images: `{rounded.none}` (0px) — Maintain photographic integrity.
- Buttons: `{rounded.xs}` (5.14286px) — Distinguish from flat text.
- Form Inputs: `{rounded.none}` (0px) — Sharp geometry aligns with brand precision.
- Badges (light): `{rounded.xs}` (5.14286px) — Subtle separation.
- Badges (dark): `{rounded.none}` (0px) — Match overlay aesthetic.
- Modals & Overlays: `{rounded.none}` (0px) — No departure from brand geometry.

### Border Widths

- **Thin (2px):** Used on outline buttons and certain interactive borders. Provides visible but restrained stroke emphasis.

## 6. Depth & Elevation

The system employs a **color-blocking** elevation strategy rather than progressive shadow layering. Depth is conveyed through surface color changes (light canvas to black band) rather than visual lift. Two shadow states exist for modal surfaces:

| Level | Treatment | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Flat | No shadow | Default cards, images, text, most containers. |
| Modal (Medium) | `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.28) 0px 6.86px 17.14px 0px` | Secondary modal or floating content; visible but restrained lift. |
| Modal (Large) | `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12) 0px 24px 105px 0px` | Primary modal or overlay; distributed soft shadow for gentle elevation. |

**Shadow Philosophy:**
The brand avoids aggressive depth cues. Rather than stacking multiple shadow layers, the system uses a single, soft shadow for modals and overlays, allowing color blocking (black sections, white canvas) to establish visual hierarchy. This restraint maintains the aesthetic's editorial quality and prevents UI chrome from competing with content imagery.

### Z-index / Layering

- **Base / Content (z: 1–5):** Standard page content, cards, and text layers.
- **Dropdown / Navigation (z: 10–20):** Navigation menus and dropdown selectors; above base content but below sticky elements.
- **Sticky (z: 999):** Header, persistent navigation; stays in viewport.
- **Modal (z: 1000–9999):** Overlay dialogs, privacy consent, modals; highest stacking order for user interaction.

## 7. Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- **Leverage the monochromatic foundation.** Use `{colors.primary}` (`#1D1D1C`) and `{colors.on-primary}` (`#FFFFFF`) for all CTA elements; this contrast is the system's core strength.
- **Scale typography deliberately.** Use display sizes (`{typography.display}` = 82.29px) for hero headlines and body sizes (`{typography.body-md}` = 15.43px) for prose; avoid intermediate sizes that dilute hierarchy.
- **Honor the spacing rhythm.** Respect `{spacing.band}` (68px) for major section breaks; do not compress sections below `{spacing.section}` (56px).
- **Prioritize imagery.** Allow full-width photos and imagery to set narrative tone; reserve UI for navigation and CTA support.
- **Use GT Alpina for secondary text.** Navigation, captions, and metadata gain editorial warmth and visual distinction when set in the classical serif.
- **Maintain sharp geometry.** Keep buttons and cards at `{rounded.none}` or `{rounded.xs}` only; avoid over-rounding that contradicts the brand's angular precision.
- **Ensure sufficient contrast.** Dark text (`#1D1D1C`) on light canvas, white text on black band—no intermediate grays that reduce readability.
- **Test at breakpoints.** Heading sizes scale significantly (35px at 375px → 82px at 1440px); verify proportional scaling across viewport widths.

### Don't

- **Introduce third colors.** The palette is deliberately limited. Do not add accent colors, status reds, or decorative hues that fragment the monochromatic aesthetic.
- **Soften the radius.** Border-radius should remain at 0px or 5.14px; avoid rounded corners that approach pill shapes unless explicitly part of the design.
- **Add drop shadows to non-modal elements.** Only modals and overlays use the soft shadow values; all standard content relies on color blocking for depth.
- **Compress typography spacing.** Do not set line-heights below 0.95 (display) or 1.0 (body); the system's openness is a defining trait.
- **Use decorative gradients or meshes.** The brand avoids gradient fills; all surfaces are flat colors from the palette.
- **Ignore the band spacing rhythm.** Section breaks should follow `{spacing.band}` (68px) to maintain visual consistency and narrative pacing.
- **Override ATC Diatype with other sans-serifs.** The geometric precision of this font is integral; substitutions risk diluting brand identity.
- **Nest modals without purpose.** Z-index layering is restrained; avoid stacking multiple overlays that obscure content.
- **Reduce button padding** below measured dimensions. The 33.42px height and deliberate padding proportions reinforce CTA prominence.

## 9. Agent Prompt Guide

### Quick Color Reference

- **Primary CTA:** Brand Accent (`{colors.primary}` — `#1D1D1C`)
- **CTA Text on Dark:** On Primary (`{colors.on-primary}` — `#FFFFFF`)
- **Default Background:** Canvas (`{colors.canvas}` — `#F9F7F5`)
- **Section Band:** Surface Alt (`{colors.surface-alt}` — `#000000`)
- **Heading Text:** Primary or Surface Alt (context-dependent; use high contrast)
- **Secondary Text:** Muted (`{colors.muted}` — `#969696`)
- **Body Copy:** Primary (`{colors.primary}` — `#1D1D1C`)
- **Captions:** Muted (`{colors.muted}` — `#969696`)

### Iteration Guide

1. **Build with monochrome hierarchy first.** Establish all layout, typography, and spacing with only `#1D1D1C`, `#FFFFFF`, and `#F9F7F5`. Verify CTA prominence and readability before adding secondary colors.

2. **Scale typography proportionally.** Use `clamp(min, vw-based, max)` for display and heading sizes so they grow/shrink smoothly across viewports. Match the measured 3.5x ratio from 375px (35px) to 1440px (82px).

3. **Honor spacing rhythm.** Every section break should be `{spacing.band}` (68px) vertically. Horizontal padding is 0px; let the container width constrain content naturally.

4. **Choose the right font.** ATC Diatype for all body, heading, and button copy (geometric, contemporary). GT Alpina for navigation, captions, and secondary text (classical, warm). Mono families only for code and labels.

5. **Button hierarchy via background, not size.** Primary buttons are dark-on-white or white-on-dark; secondary and ghost variants reduce visual weight through fill or outline removal, not size reduction. All buttons measure 33.42px height.

6. **No drops shadows except modals.** Color blocking via surface changes establishes depth. Reserve shadow values for modal overlays only.

7. **Responsive: test at 375px, 768px, 1024px, 1440px.** Content width scales (340px → 1371px); typography and spacing scale proportionally. No fixed breakpoint jumps; use fluid scaling with clamp().

8. **Round only interactive elements.** Buttons and small badges: `{rounded.xs}` (5.14286px). Everything else (images, cards, modals): `{rounded.none}` (0px). Do not approximate; use exact values.

9. **Link styling via underline and GT Alpina weight.** Links are light weight (`300`) GT Alpina in the on-primary color; underline signals interactivity. No color shift on hover; rely on semantic underline.

10. **Verify coverage with placeholder copy.** With each component build, confirm display headings scale correctly, body text maintains legibility at all sizes, and buttons remain prominent. The design is measured; implementation must match precision.

## 10. Known Gaps

- **Interaction states not fully specified.** The extraction captured many focus/hover/active CSS rules, but these were largely browser defaults (`:focus-ring-color`, `:focus-visible` outlines) and plugin-specific variables. No custom, design-intent hover states (e.g., button color shift on hover) were clearly measured and attributed to the brand design; their absence should be confirmed with the brand before implementation.

- **No error/success/warning colors.** The site does not expose semantic status colors in its markup or measured styles. If validation states or feedback messaging is required, a status ramp must be inferred or sourced from the brand.

- **Opacity and transparency values** (e.g., for disabled states, overlays, or hover effects) were not systematically extracted. The `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` ghost badge and a few other rgba fills were captured, but a complete opacity scale is not available.

- **Dark mode or theme variants not analyzed.** Only one viewport/theme was captured. If the brand ships a dark mode, light mode variant, or season-specific palette, it was not measured.

- **Gradients and decorative textures.** The extraction found no gradients, meshes, or pattern fills. The site may use them in hero backgrounds or as full-bleed overlays, but they were not reported as design tokens. If present, they require separate documentation.

- **Two colors marked unassigned** (`{colors.neutral-1}` `#444444` and `{colors.neutral-2}` `#E0DEDC`). These were present in extracted elements but had no clear semantic role. They are described as decorative in the Color Palette section, but their intended use should be confirmed.

- **Z-index layering scale partially observed.** Values 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 999, 1000, 9999 were captured, but context for each is limited. Custom z-index collisions or nesting scenarios were not tested.

- **Only 1 page analyzed** (homepage). Surfaces behind authentication, account pages, booking flows, or specialized sections were not visited or measured.

- **Component interaction animations** (e.g., button transitions, hover effects, focus ring animations) were not extracted. The system may animate CTA fill changes or opacity shifts; these would require separate motion documentation.

- **Form input states** (disabled, error, placeholder text color, focus ring style) were partially extracted but not fully systematized into a usable input component spec.

- **Responsive images and video embeds.** Aspect ratios, lazy-loading, and srcset strategies were not measured. Hero images appear to scale fluidly, but exact breakpoint behavior is inferred, not confirmed.
